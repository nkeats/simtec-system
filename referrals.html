Referrals arrive from the rewards app and route straight to the consultant who sold the person doing the referring. Booking an appointment against the same mobile number marks it Appointment booked on its own. If nothing is booked within 14 days it comes back here flagged With office — reassign it and that consultant gets a fresh 14 days. The 50,000-point reward pays itself once the referred customer has bought and either put down a 20% deposit, paid in full, or made 5 consecutive payments.
