Paysmart balance import

What this is for

Around 80 Australian customers still pay through Paysmart rather than Ezidebit. They do not appear in the daily Ezidebit report, so nothing in the system knew about them. This brings them in alongside the Ezidebit book.

Drop in Paysmart's balances export — the one with AccountReference, ContractValue, Paid, Balance.

Paysmart customers are kept separate. They are flagged as their own provider and matched only against other Paysmart records, so the two books never blur together and you can always say what each company is collecting.
