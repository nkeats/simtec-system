The conditions

  1. A referral is a person whose name, suburb and phone number were given to us by you, who agreed to have a presentation and who then purchased a Simtec mattress. Points and prizes are credited to your account once that person has either paid for their mattress in full or made at least 2 Ezidebit payments.
  2. Every referral earns you 50,000 points, with three exceptions. On your 5th, 10th and 25th referral you receive the prize set out below instead of the 50,000 points for that referral.
  3. Your 5th referral earns a $100 voucher instead of the 50,000 points. The $100 voucher works the same way as a $50 voucher: take it as a voucher, use it to reduce the amount owing on your plan, or donate it to your group if that group is signed up with Simtec.
  4. Your 10th referral earns the bedding bundle instead of the 50,000 points. The bedding bundle comprises 2 pillows and a mattress protector.
  5. Your 25th referral makes you a Super Referrer and earns a holiday instead of the 50,000 points. The holiday is valued at $2,000 and the destination is at the discretion of Simtec Therapeutic.
