1. General
These Terms and Conditions of Sale ("Agreement") govern the purchase of a Simtec Therapeutic mattress ("Product") and are binding upon the purchaser ("Customer") upon execution of the order form.
(a) These Terms and Conditions are given to the Customer before the order form is signed, and form part of the Agreement. A copy is left with the Customer at the time of sale.

2. Manufacturer's Warranty
All Simtec Therapeutic mattresses are covered by a fifteen (15) year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, backed by the original equipment manufacturer ("OEM"). This warranty applies only to faults arising from the manufacture of the Product.
(a) The warranty does not cover damage, deterioration, soiling or loss of performance caused by factors outside the manufacturing process, including misuse, neglect or failure to follow the care instructions; accidental damage; staining, spills, burns, cuts, tears or soiling; unhygienic use; damage caused by an inadequate or unsupportive base or frame; normal wear and tear; or any alteration, modification or repair not authorised in writing by the manufacturer.
(b) The warranty is administered through the manufacturer and is non-transferable, applying to the original purchaser only unless otherwise stated in writing by the manufacturer.
(c) Nothing in this clause limits or excludes any rights the Customer has under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993.

3. Comfort Guarantee
Mk III Queen and Super King mattresses are covered by a thirty (30) day comfort guarantee.
(a) The full terms of the comfort guarantee are given to the Customer with these Terms and Conditions before the order form is signed, and are also emailed to the Customer on confirmation of sale.

4. Cooling-Off Period — Uninvited Sales
Where the purchase has arisen from an uninvited approach by a Company representative, the Customer has five (5) working days to cancel under the Fair Trading Act 1986.
(a) The five working days run from the day the Customer receives a copy of this Agreement. The Customer's consultant explains this right to the Customer before the order form is signed.
(b) The Customer may cancel for any reason, by telling the Company in writing, by phone on 09 886 9897, by email to info@simtectp.com, or by using the cancellation form provided with the order. The Customer does not have to give a reason.
(c) If the Customer cancels within the five working days, the Company will refund everything the Customer has paid, in full, within 5 working days, and will collect the Product at the Company's own cost if it has already been delivered.
(d) The Company may accept payment and may deliver the Product during the five working days. Doing so does not affect the Customer's right to cancel or to a full refund.

5. Solicited Sales
Whether the cooling-off period in clause 4 applies depends on whether the Customer personally invited the Company to attend.
(a) Where the Customer contacted the Company, or asked the Company to visit or call them, the sale is a solicited sale and clause 4 does not apply. The Company records how and when the Customer made that request.
(b) In every other case clause 4 applies and the Customer has the cooling-off period. Where another person referred the Customer, the Company records that referral so any referral reward can be paid.

6. Deferred Delivery — Direct Debit Payments
Where the Customer pays by direct debit, delivery will not occur until the total amount paid is at least ten percent (10%) of the total purchase price.
(a) The Company may also defer delivery where payments have been missed or dishonoured. If the Company defers delivery for this reason it will tell the Customer in writing, say why, and say what is needed before delivery will proceed.
(b) The Company will not continue to take payments for more than 8 weeks beyond the 10% threshold without either delivering the Product or offering the Customer a full refund.

7. Cancellation by the Company Before Delivery
The Company may cancel the Customer's order before delivery where payments have been missed or dishonoured and the Customer has not remedied this after being asked to.
(a) Where the Company cancels, it may retain from monies paid a reasonable amount to recover its actual administrative costs, up to a maximum of five hundred dollars (NZD $500.00). The balance is refunded to the Customer.
(b) The Company will give the Customer a written breakdown of any amount it retains. The Company retains only what it has actually spent, and does not retain any amount as a profit or penalty.
(c) Where the Customer demonstrates that cancellation has arisen from genuine financial hardship, evidenced by reasonable documentation, the Company will give favourable consideration to a full refund of all monies paid.

8. Binding Contract
By executing the order form the Customer enters into a legally binding contract for the purchase of the Product.
(a) On expiry of any cooling-off period under clause 4, the full purchase price remains due and payable in accordance with the agreed payment terms.
(b) Nothing in this clause affects the Customer's rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, the Fair Trading Act 1986, or the Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017, including any right to cancel where the Product is faulty or where the Customer was misled.

9. Continuing Payment Obligation
Following delivery the Customer remains liable for the full outstanding balance of the purchase price, which continues to be due and payable in accordance with the agreed payment terms. Delivery does not discharge, reduce or otherwise affect that obligation.
(a) The Customer must continue making all scheduled payments until the total purchase price is paid in full. Failure to do so is a default under this Agreement.
(b) If the Customer is having difficulty paying, they should contact the Company. The Company will consider a payment arrangement.

10. Recovery of Costs on Default
If the Customer does not pay an amount when it is due and the Company has to take steps to recover it, the Customer must pay the reasonable costs the Company actually incurs in recovering it.
(a) Those costs are limited to the fees a debt collection agency or lawyer actually charges the Company for recovering the Customer's account, and any costs a court or tribunal orders the Customer to pay.
(b) The Company charges the Customer only what the Company is actually charged. The Company does not add any margin, commission or administration charge of its own, and does not recover the cost of pursuing any other customer's account.
(c) The Company will tell the Customer the amount before adding it to the balance, and will give a breakdown on request.
(d) The Company does not charge interest on late payments. The Company does not charge any fee for entering into, administering, or ending the Customer's purchase.
(e) Whether the Customer must pay any court or tribunal costs is a matter for the court or tribunal to decide.
(f) The Company may pursue recovery of the outstanding balance, including by a claim before the Disputes Tribunal or another court or tribunal of competent jurisdiction in New Zealand.

11. Credit Reporting
If the Customer defaults and the default is not remedied, the Company may disclose that default to a credit reporter.
(a) The Company may give a credit reporter the Customer's name, contact details, the amount in default and the status of the account. Credit reporters operating in New Zealand include Centrix, Equifax and illion.
(b) A default listing remains on the Customer's credit record for five years and may affect the Customer's ability to obtain credit.
(c) Before listing a default the Company will write to the Customer at their last known address, tell them the payment is overdue, and ask them to pay.
(d) If the Customer pays the amount, the Company will tell the credit reporter so the listing is updated.
(e) The Company may also give a credit reporter or debt collection agency the Customer's information for the purpose of recovering the debt.

12. Privacy
The Company collects the Customer's personal information to supply the Product, to administer payments, and to recover any amount not paid.
(a) The information is held by Simtec Therapeutic Limited, 297 Neilson Street, Onehunga, Auckland, in accordance with the Privacy Act 2020.
(b) The Customer may ask to see the information the Company holds about them and may ask for it to be corrected, by contacting 09 886 9897 or info@simtectp.com.
(c) The Company may give the Customer's information to a credit reporter or debt collection agency as set out in clause 11, and to its delivery contractors for the purpose of delivering the Product.
