Australian contract import

What this is for

Ezidebit's contract export (the SR-…….csv they send) is the only source that carries what each customer still owes. The arrears report does not — it only shows what was billed and collected, which is why the outstanding book could never be worked out from it.

Drop the file in below. Every payer is matched on their Ezidebit payer ID. Someone already on file keeps their arrears history and simply gains the contract side; someone new is added.

It will not touch paid, arrears, expected or instalment. Those come from the arrears report and this file has no authority over them. It only writes the contract side — balance owing, payments remaining, next payment, status and contact details.
