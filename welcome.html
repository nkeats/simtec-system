Open this on your phone

Your rewards app lives on your phone, so the rest of this needs to happen there. Point your phone's camera at this square and tap the link that pops up.

Or open your email on your phone and tap the same link you tapped here.

How to save it, step by step

About thirty seconds. You only do this once.

  1. Tap the button below and sign in

    Put in your mobile number and we will text you a six-digit code. It has to be the number that is on your Simtec order. No password, ever.

  2. Now save it to your phone

    You are in. The last thing is to put it on your home screen so it opens with one tap.

    Came here from Facebook or your email? Saving to the home screen only works in Safari or Chrome. Tap the three dots (or the square) in the corner, choose Open in browser, and carry on from here.

  3. Find the Share button

    One of these two is on your screen. If you cannot see it, tap the three dots first and you will find it in there.

    iPhone usually along the bottom
    Android top right corner

  4. Tap “Add to Home Screen”

    A list slides up. Scroll down it until you see Add to Home Screen. On some Android phones it says Install app instead — same thing.

    Then tap Add in the top corner, or Install on an Android.

  5. That is it

    The Simtec coin is now on your home screen with your other apps. Open it from there from now on.
